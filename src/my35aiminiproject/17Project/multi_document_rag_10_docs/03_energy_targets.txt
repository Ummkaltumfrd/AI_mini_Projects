Energy Targets and Definitions
==============================

2025 Target
-----------
Atlas Energy aims to reduce total electricity consumption by 8% in 2025 compared with the 2024 baseline.

Renewable Target
----------------
The company aims for at least 45% of annual electricity consumption to be supplied by on-site renewable generation by the end of 2026.

Definition
----------
Renewable share is calculated as on-site renewable electricity generated and used divided by total facility electricity consumption, multiplied by 100. For the demo's simple calculation, assume all solar generation is used on-site.
